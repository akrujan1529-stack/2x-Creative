SELECTED PROJECT / 2026

VANTA COFFEE.

A bold AI-powered visual campaign created to make a coffee brand feel premium, modern and impossible to ignore.

CLIENT Vanta Coffee
INDUSTRY Food & Beverage
DELIVERABLES Product Visuals / Advertising
APPROACH AI × Art Direction × Design
01

THE CHALLENGE

MAKE A SIMPLE PRODUCT FEEL PREMIUM.

Coffee is a crowded category. The challenge was to create a visual identity that could instantly separate Vanta from ordinary product photography and give the brand a stronger digital presence.

CREATIVE DIRECTION

DARK. BOLD. UNFORGETTABLE.

We combined dramatic lighting, rich contrast and cinematic composition to create a premium coffee world around the product.

THE 2X APPROACH

PRODUCT FIRST.

01

ATTENTION

Create an immediate visual hook that works in a fast-scrolling social environment.

02

DESIRE

Use premium visual language to make the product feel desirable and high-value.

03

MEMORY

Build a distinctive visual world people can associate with the brand.

PROJECT OUTCOME

FROM ORDINARY TO 2X.

A premium product campaign designed for social media, advertising and future brand communication.
